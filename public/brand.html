Brand sheet

NYNZ brand basics.

One page for anyone making something with the NYNZ name on it.

Logo

A bold NYNZ wordmark with one orange orbit rising around the top right of the Z. It should always read as moving up and forward. Letters and orbit are hand-drawn SVG paths, so the logo has no font dependency. Use light versions on Paper or white and dark versions on Navy.

Horizontal, light. Primary logo.

Horizontal, dark. Used in the site header and footer.

Stacked, light. For square spaces.

Stacked, dark.

Icon, light: the N with the orbit.

App icon and favicon: white N and orbit on a navy tile.

The name is NYNZ, always in capitals, never extended or combined with another word. Keep clear space equal to the height of the N's top half on every side. Do not flip or rotate the orbit so that it points down, recolour it, add effects or outlines, or stretch the logo. Files: horizontal, stacked, icon, social image.

Colour

Navy, signal orange and white. Orange on navy measures 5.6:1, which passes AA, but keep it to accents, buttons and large text. on light backgrounds use Ember for any orange text, because Signal orange on white is 3.1:1 (large text only). Buttons are orange with navy text (5.6:1). Ratios are WCAG 2 values.
