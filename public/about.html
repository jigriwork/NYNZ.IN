About

One job, done carefully: clean catalogue data.

NYNZ is a new company based in Berhampur, Odisha, India, founded by Adib Sattar. We start with a free, read-only health audit of your product catalogue.

What NYNZ is

Catalogue and feed care for Shopify stores.

Product data goes wrong slowly: a missing barcode here, a stale price there, a feed item rejected and never looked at again. NYNZ finds those problems and reports them.

The audit comes first, and it is free. If you want more, there is a detailed audit and, in early access, ongoing monitoring. We do not build themes, run advertising or promise marketplace approvals.

How the work is done

Software and AI do the work. You make the decisions.

Software and AI read

They read the export or feed you share and report what they find, with examples.

Findings are automated

We do not claim a person checks every finding, and automated findings can be wrong. We say so in every report.

You approve any change

The audit changes nothing. If you later choose monitoring, nothing goes live without your approval, and batches can be rolled back.
