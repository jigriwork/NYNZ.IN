Get your free health audit
Is the free audit really free?

Yes. There is no charge and no obligation, and we do not ask for payment details. It is available for India and international stores.

What do I get?

A summary of findings: counts by problem type, examples from your own data, and a note of what the data you shared did not let us check. It is a report only.

What do you need from me?

Your store link or platform, your country, and ideally a product export (for Shopify, a CSV from admin) or a feed file you already have. We do not need passwords and we do not ask for store access at this stage.

Do you change my store?

No. The audit is read-only. It changes nothing and publishes nothing.

What does the detailed audit cost?

In India it is ₹1,999 + GST. For international stores it is early access, with pricing confirmed after your free audit.

Is GST added?

India prices are shown before GST, which is added where it applies.

What happens after the free audit?

You decide. Nothing continues unless you ask. If you want every problem listed with a proposed fix, ask about the detailed audit. If you want ongoing care, ask about monitoring.

When is monitoring available?

Monitoring is early access. We have tested our process only on example data in a local test, not on a live store. Pricing is confirmed after your free audit, and we will tell you honestly whether we can take you on.

Will monitoring change my store without asking?

No. Nothing goes live without your approval, given in the review queue, a shared sheet or by email as agreed, and nothing is applied automatically. Silence is not approval. Every published batch keeps the previous state so it can be restored.

Can you guarantee my products will be approved by Google or a marketplace?

No. Google and marketplaces make their own decisions and change their rules. We report what the data shows and what remains.

How do you use AI?

AI and software read the data, find problems and draft fixes. In monitoring, a separate checking agent verifies every proposal, and the agent that drafts a fix never approves it. Where a finding depends on facts such as price, stock, SKU, barcode or product identity, it needs evidence such as your supplier file. Otherwise it is flagged.

Does a person at NYNZ check every finding?

No. The analysis and checking are automated, and we do not claim otherwise. Automated findings can be wrong, so look at each example. For any change to your store, your approval is the human decision.

Which platforms do you support?

Shopify stores, with Google Merchant Center and eBay file feeds. These are the only integrations we have tested so far, and only on example data in a local test. Tell us if you use something else and we will say honestly whether we can help.
