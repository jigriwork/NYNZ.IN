FAQ

Questions we are asked.

Do you need access to my Shopify store?

Not to start. The health check and setup can work from a product export. For ongoing work we ask for a staff account limited to products and inventory, never owner access.

Will you change anything without asking?

No. Nothing goes live on your store without your approval, given in the review queue, a shared sheet or by email as agreed, and nothing is applied automatically. Silence is not approval.

Can you guarantee my products will be approved by Google or a marketplace?

No. Google and marketplaces make their own decisions and change their rules. We fix the data they report problems with and tell you what remains.

What if a change is wrong?

Every published batch keeps the previous state, so it can be restored. Tell us and we will roll it back and look at why it happened.

How do you use AI?

AI and software find problems, draft fixes and check them. A separate checking agent verifies every proposal, and the agent that drafts a fix never approves it. Where a change depends on facts such as price, stock, SKU, barcode or product identity, it needs authoritative evidence, such as your supplier file. Otherwise it is flagged for you. Nothing is published without your approval.

Does a person at NYNZ check every change?

No. The checking is automated, and we do not claim otherwise. Your approval is the human decision in the process. That is why nothing is applied without it, why uncertain items are flagged, and why every published batch can be rolled back. Automated checks can be wrong, so look at each before and after.

Which platforms do you support?

Shopify stores, with Google Merchant Center and eBay file feeds. These are the only integrations we have tested so far, and only on example data in a local test, not on a live store. Other marketplaces are not supported yet. Ask if you need something else and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it.

Is there a contract?

Yes, a signed client agreement. Starter is monthly with a 2-month minimum. India Pro is monthly with no lock-in. All plans have a one-time setup fee.
