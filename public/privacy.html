Business contacts we find ourselves

We may contact people at businesses we think could use our service, by email or LinkedIn message, using work details they or their business have made public. Our sources are company websites, Companies House (the UK register of companies), Shopify App Store reviews that name a store, and LinkedIn profiles we view by hand. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in offering a business service. Every such message tells you where we found your details and how to opt out, and you can object at any time. We keep prospect records for at most 6 months after our last message if you do not reply, then delete them. If you opt out, we keep a minimal record (email or company domain, reason and date) so we do not contact you again. [Lawful basis under India's DPDP Act, and for US contacts, to be confirmed.]
