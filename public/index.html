How the audit works

Three steps, and nothing changes in your store.

We work from the export, feed file or store link you share. You decide what happens next.

  1. Share

    Email us your store link and country, and a product export or feed file if you have one.

  2. We read it

    Software and AI read the data. Read-only: nothing in your store is touched.

  3. You get a summary

    Counts by problem type, with examples from your own data. Free.

What the audit looks at

The data problems that stores keep putting off.

Barcodes and identifiers

Missing or malformed GTINs, which feeds reject first.

Titles and attributes

Stray capitals, symbols and missing fields that feeds flag.

Variants and duplicates

Broken option sets and listings that appear twice.

Stock and price

Where you share a supplier file, mismatches are flagged, never guessed.

Shopping feeds

Items that Google Merchant Center or an eBay file would reject, where you share the feed or its errors.

What we could not check

The summary says what the data you shared did not let us confirm.
