What we look after

The unglamorous data work, done properly.

Most stores know their catalogue has problems. Few have someone whose job is to fix them every week.

  • Product dataTitles, attributes, variants, barcodes (GTINs) and duplicate listings.
  • Stock and price checksSupplier stock files matched to your SKUs, with mismatches and out-of-stock items still on sale flagged.
  • Shopping feedsGoogle Merchant Center and one marketplace feed checked weekly, with rejected items fixed.
  • New listingsDrafted from your supplier sheets, checked automatically and sent to you for approval before they are published.
  • A monthly health reportWhat we found, what we changed and what still needs your input.

How it works

Proposed, checked, approved, then published.

1. Detect and propose

Software and AI read your catalogue export and feed error reports, sort every problem by type and draft a fix for each, with the old value, the new value and the evidence.

2. Independent check

A separate checker, never the one that drafted the fix, tests each proposal. Anything it cannot confirm is flagged for you rather than proposed as fixed.

3. You approve, we publish

You see each change as a before and after. Only changes you approve go live, with a saved copy of the previous state, so any batch can be undone.
