How it works

Audit first. Changes only if you ask, and only with your approval.

The free health audit is read-only. Software and AI read the data you share and report what they find. Nothing is changed or published in your store.

The audit

From your export to a summary of findings.

Free, read-only and the same for India and international stores.

  1. Share

    Email us your store link, platform and country. Add a product export or a feed file if you have one (for Shopify, a CSV from admin). Please do not send passwords. We do not ask for store access at this stage.

  2. We read

    Software and AI read what you shared, and your public store pages where that helps, and sort every problem by type: missing barcodes, broken variants, titles feeds would flag, price or stock mismatches against a supplier file you share.

  3. Summary of findings

    You get a short report: counts by problem type, examples from your own data, and what the data you shared did not let us check. It is free, and nothing is changed or published.

  4. Detailed audit (optional)

    The detailed audit lists each problem with a proposed fix and the evidence for it. It is still read-only. In India it costs ₹1,999 + GST. For international stores it is early access, with pricing confirmed after your free audit.

Detailed audit

Every problem, with a proposed fix and its evidence.

Each row says what field is affected, what it is now, what it would become and the evidence behind it. Where there is no evidence, the item is flagged rather than guessed.

Monitoring · early access

If you want ongoing care, every change still waits for you.

Monitoring is an early access service, and our process has been tested only on example data in a local test, not on a live store. Pricing is confirmed after your free audit.

  1. Detect

    Read the catalogue and feed errors and sort problems by type.

  2. Propose

    Draft one fix per problem, with old value, new value and evidence.

  3. Independent check

    A separate checker tests every proposal. It never checks its own work.

  4. Your approval

    You approve or reject. Nothing goes live without it.

  5. Publish and verify

    Publish approved changes, save the previous state, re-check.

Your decision

You choose what goes live.

Select the changes you accept. Flagged items stay out until you decide. Publishing saves the previous state first, so a batch can be restored. Silence is never approval.
