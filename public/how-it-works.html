How it works

A clear process, with your approval before anything goes live.

Software and AI do the finding, drafting and checking. A separate automated checker verifies every proposal. You approve every change before it goes live on your store.

  1. Set up

    You share a product export or limited staff access, your supplier stock files and the feeds you sell through. We propose a one-time cleanup for your approval, then agree who on your side approves changes.

  2. Detect

    Each week we read the catalogue and feed error reports and sort every problem by type: missing barcodes, broken variants, price mismatches, policy words, stock that is out but still on sale.

  3. Propose

    Software and AI draft each fix as a single change with the old value, the new value, the evidence used and how confident the match is. Nothing is applied automatically.

  4. Independent check

    A separate checking agent tests every proposal. The agent that drafts a fix never approves it. Fixed rules check that each change is well formed. For prices, stock, SKUs, barcodes and product identity, a change needs authoritative evidence, such as your supplier file or your own records. Without it, the item is flagged for you and no fix is proposed.

  5. Your approval

    We send you the change set through the approval method in your agreement: the review queue, a shared sheet or email. You approve or reject each change, or approve a whole change set you have seen. Silence is not approval, and without your approval nothing is published.

  6. Publish and check

    Only the changes you approved are published, with a saved copy of the previous state. We then re-run the checks to see whether the error count went down, and flag anything that did not improve.

  7. Monthly report

    You get a one-page summary: problems found, changes made, items waiting on you, and anything we could not fix. Any batch can be rolled back.

Safeguards

Built to be undone.

  • Nothing auto-appliedEvery change waits for your approval, given in the review queue, a shared sheet or by email. Low-confidence matches and destructive changes are flagged for extra care.
  • Two keys for stockA supplier stock update is only proposed when the SKU match is confirmed by barcode or product title. Uncertain matches are flagged, and you still approve every update.
  • RollbackEvery published batch keeps the previous state so it can be restored.
  • Honest limitsAutomated checks can be wrong. That is why you approve every change, why uncertain items are flagged instead of fixed, and why every batch can be rolled back.
  • Limited accessWe work from exports or restricted staff accounts, never full owner access.
