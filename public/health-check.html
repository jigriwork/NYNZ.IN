What to expect

Simple, free, and nothing changes in your store.

What you get

A summary of findings: counts by problem type, examples from your own data, and what the data you shared did not let us check.

What we need

Your store link or platform, your country, and ideally a product export or feed file. Passwords are never needed.

What we do not do

We do not log in to your store, change anything or publish anything. No store access is requested at this stage.

What happens next

You stay in control.

  1. You email us

    Use the button above. It opens an email with the details we need filled in as a template.

  2. We reply

    We confirm what we need. For Shopify, a product CSV exported from admin is enough.

  3. You get the summary

    We send the summary of findings. We cannot promise what an audit will find, and automated findings can be wrong.

  4. You decide

    Nothing continues unless you ask. The detailed audit costs ₹1,999 + GST in India, and is early access with pricing confirmed after your free audit for international stores. Monitoring is early access, with pricing confirmed after your free audit.

Not included

What the audit is not.

  • An audit of your theme, ads or marketplace account health
  • A promise that Google or a marketplace will approve a listing
  • Any change to your store

We use the details you send only to reply and prepare your audit. If we ask for an export, send product data only, never customer or order data. See our privacy policy (draft).
